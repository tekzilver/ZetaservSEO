## Overview

**ZetaServ SEO** brings technical diagnostics, prioritized SEO execution roadmaps, AI-grounded content & keyword research, Video SEO scripting, AI search readiness (`llms.txt`), and scheduled social email digests together into one calm, fast workspace.

Built for founders, website owners, and small teams managing one domain or up to 15 websites without agency bloat.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Live Technical & On-Page Site Crawler
- Crawls your site pages in real time and calculates an overall **On-Page Health Score** with **TTFB / response speed** diagnostics.
- Audits `<title>` tags, meta descriptions, `H1` hierarchy, canonical tags, Open Graph tags, missing image `alt` attributes, internal links, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml`.
- Includes ready-to-copy **HTML & Schema.org JSON-LD fix templates** for detected issues.

### 2. Daily, Weekly & Monthly SEO Action Plans
- Converts real crawl findings into structured, plain-English **Daily**, **Weekly**, and **Monthly** checklists.
- Prioritizes tasks by SEO impact (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`) and category (`Technical`, `On-Page`, `Content`, `Video`, `Social`) with trackable completion progress.

### 3. Content, Keyword & Page Optimizer Studio
- **Keyword Research:** Structured keyword tables (primary, long-tail, question queries, search intent) mapped to your existing pages.
- **Full SEO Articles:** Publication-ready Markdown articles with optimized title tags, meta descriptions, heading hierarchy, FAQs, and internal link ideas.
- **Competitor SERP Gap Spy:** Identifies missing subtopics, buyer questions, and E-E-A-T angles needed to outrank competing pages.
- **Existing Page Optimizer:** Fetches live on-page signals for any URL and rewrites `<title>`, `<meta>`, `H1`, structure, and JSON-LD schema.
- **JSON-LD Schema Generator:** Production-ready structured data snippets for `Article`, `FAQPage`, `Organization`, and `WebSite`.

### 4. Video SEO Script & Storyboard Studio
- Generates **16:9 Explainer** (90s) and **9:16 Shorts / Reels / TikTok** (45s) scripts with 3-second attention hooks and teleprompter `[PAUSE]` cues.
- Outputs shot-by-shot visual storyboards, ready-to-upload **`.srt` subtitle files**, optimized YouTube title/description/tags, and embeddable **`VideoObject` JSON-LD**.

### 5. Automated Social & Email Digests
- Generates tailored social posts for **LinkedIn, X, Instagram, and Facebook** grounded in your site's focus keywords and brand tone.
- Supports **Daily** or **Weekly** schedules with live HTML email previews and automated inbox delivery.

### 6. AI Search Readiness (`llms.txt` / GEO)
- Automatically drafts and exports a clean **`llms.txt`** summary tailored to your domain so modern AI search assistants accurately index your key pages.

### 7. Role-Based Workspace Controls
- Google Sign-In authentication with workspace switcher, brand tone & audience settings, and team collaborator permissions.
